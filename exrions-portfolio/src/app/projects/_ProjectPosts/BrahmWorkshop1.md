Brahm Coding Workshop 1 - A voluntary workshop hosted in collaboration with Brahm Centre Singapore.

This was my very first time planning and hosting a workshop for a non-profit! This was done in collaboration with Brahm Centre Singapore to bring the world of coding to a wider audience. 
# Introduction
I've always loved coding ever since I first started learning it as a kid. My first time properly interacting with coding was in primary school, where I got introduced to Scrath and Lego Mindstorms.
![Lego Mindstorms Programming](/posts/BrahmWorkshop1_LegoMindstorms.png)
It then sparked my continued interest in drag and drop style programming till I finished secondary school. After which I entered polytechnic, with a diploma in information technology to obtain. 

This eventually led me to my interest in game development and my wish to share this very passion with others! 

And thanks to my mum and her company's frequent events and contributions to Brahm Centre, I got to know about the non-profit organisation and get in contact with them to host this very workshop.
# The Workshop
## Icebreaker
For this workshop, I began with an icebreaker. In the form of a bingo. It was pretty hard to plan what items to put in there since I didn't know the participant age range just yet. 

This icebreaker was met with some issues due to my own lack of foresight as well, such as the fact that it was not possible to annotate with pens! Curse my love of dark themes 😔
![Ice Breaker Worksheet](/posts/BrahmWorkshop1_Bingo.png)
## Content
This workshop was planned with the absolute basics in mind, yet with a very tight timeline to do so. I had two 2 hour sessions to cover the basics of coding: variables, operators, and conditionals. 

The slides were made with a coding example in mind, so they are minimal and have little content since most of it is done during the workshop itself!

## Running the workshop
I also managed to invite my friend Nick to join me in the workshop! Together we taught the participants the basics of C#!
![Workshop Picture](/posts/BrahmWorkshop1_InAction.jpeg)
It was a really fun experience to learn and discover along with the attendees about the workshop. They learned coding skills and I learned so much about teaching, organising, time management, and more. It was a really good experience and I look forward to hosting more workshops with Brahm Centre.
# Outcome
This workshop has a small turn out due to internal changes at Brahm Centre and a lack of time to advertise the workshop to a wider audience. However, the smaller group was perfect as it was my first time running any kind of workshop and it gave me the experience needed to improve on my future workshops. 

Nick helped out massively thanks to his prior experience with voluntary workshops and his technical background. Do check him out if you have the time!
